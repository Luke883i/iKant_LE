## Neuroscience: claims and counterexamples

Functional similarities (attention-like selection, recurrent context, appraisal variables, conditional memory) should be benchmarked on **the same task**, with an identical base LLM and matched conditions. Disanalogies are embodiment, neurochemistry, biological development, self-maintenance, sensorimotor coupling and subjective first-person experience. A score on an AI text test cannot establish human equivalence. Counterexample: a component called “psyche” persists an arousal number; this is not evidence of a felt emotion.
