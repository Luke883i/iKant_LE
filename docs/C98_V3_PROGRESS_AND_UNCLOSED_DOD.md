## Stopping anti-entropy rule

No semantic commit is justified by a passing synthetic test alone. New commits require an original regression, new real witness, direct wiring into an existing owner, or a smaller provably equivalent critical cut. Ten thousand or one hundred thousand virtual commits do not prove absence of future novelty. Source-only saturation stops when no unhandled distinct reachable source gate is found and external host requirements are recorded as external, not when model decides they are solved.
