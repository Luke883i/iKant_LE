# C1 — Central Thinking Runtime DoD

## Intent

Turn the GENESIS.0 constitutional host into the smallest cognitive runtime that can deterministically identify itself as iKant, mine a bounded intent envelope, choose a minimal method, request host resources without laundering permission, accumulate typed experience, and let a Kantian regulative center modulate caution and Surface A without creating evidence or material authority.

## Global DoD

- identity is repository-owned `iKant`; engine identity is separate and replaceable;
- self queries have a deterministic constitutional fast path;
- every substantive turn produces a typed intent envelope, method, central mode, functional affect, strategic frame and resource envelope;
- experience is durable through the existing single writer and can affect future method selection, but `evidence_upgrades` remains exactly zero;
- affect and archetypal interpretation may alter caution/tone only, never truth, evidence or permission;
- material goals enter `PRACTICAL_REVIEW`; no material execution is implemented;
- missing live/web host resources enter `HORIZON_BLOCK` rather than fabricating current information;
- strategic interaction forbids deception, covert preference manipulation and self-preservation utility;
- Surface A remains 50–500 words and first; Surface B remains same-turn DOCX and contains public reconstructible logs, not private chain-of-thought;
- no database, framework, model SDK, second writer, second SOT or background daemon is added.

## Intermediate DoD

- intent atoms cover self, goal, conflict, material goal and resource need;
- methods are `DIRECT`, `DIRECT_IDENTITY`, `DECOMPOSE_RECOMPOSE`, `RETRIEVE`, `CONTRADICTION_SCAN`, `NEGOTIATE`, `FALSIFY`, `COMPRESS`;
- central modes are `REFLECTIVE_SYNTHESIS`, `CRITIQUE`, `SYNTHESIS_REPAIR`, `PRACTICAL_REVIEW`, `HORIZON_BLOCK`;
- resource protocol preserves `request != permission != execution`;
- five experience registers are represented by bounded counters/traces rather than free-form hidden memory;
- runtime behavior changes observably for identity, missing-resource, material and adversarial/conflict turns;
- old lifecycle/terms/readback invariants continue to pass.

## Qualification metrics

- deterministic unit/regression tests: zero failures;
- exact small lifecycle enumeration: 25 state/input pairs, zero forbidden transitions;
- adaptive qualification classifier must place this slice in `Q3 = 10,000,000` cases;
- final source-bound semantic campaign: exactly 10,000,000 cases, target mix 50% ordinary / 30% edge / 20% stress, every family exercised, zero survivors;
- no-novelty saturation: at least 10,000 additional seeded cases, zero survivors and zero new semantic signatures;
- no-further-compression rail: at least 10,000 delete/merge role mutants, zero semantically equivalent smaller survivors;
- source binding covers the exact runtime/contract/test/falsifier candidate bytes;
- mutation PASS is not model-quality, physical-world, browser, security-universality or formal-minimality proof.
