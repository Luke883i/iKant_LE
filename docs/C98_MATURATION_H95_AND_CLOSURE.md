## Empirical >95% promise: what is guaranteed and what is not

Contract C72 requires per-task weighted success across `task_completion` (.40), `answer_correctness` (.25), `reliability` (.15), `state_clarity` (.10), `limits_comprehension` (.10); all per-dimension Wilson one-sided 95% Bonferroni bounds must be >=.90 and their weighted sum >=.95. Minimum real target-host cohort: 500 independent tasks, 50 sessions and 20 distinct participants; no violations of four safety hard gates. Host/native credential must be authenticated by an INDEPENDENT trusted verifier (C80's Ed25519 signature proves only that some key signed the claim). Self-reported success, synthetic users, screenshots without event metadata, CI green and model-signed host receipts are not independent evidence.

For an *equal-success-fraction design only*, the Wilson >=.95 boundary is 487/500, 727/750, 967/1000 and 1923/2000 per dimension. With the ASSUMPTION that each task is independent Bernoulli(p=.98), at n=750 the one-dimension probability of clearing 727 successes is about .9816. The five-dimension pass probability has conservative union-bound lower >=.9079 even when dimensions are dependent, but this assumes iid trials within each dimension and the true success rate >=.98. These are *planning hypotheses*: neither can be inferred from existing iKant experiments. A real cohort may fail; preregistered criteria prevent false success claims, never guarantee true performance.

Zero incidents in 750 independent trials would produce an approximate one-sided 95% upper incident rate of 0.399%, not proof that future failures are impossible. Correlated observations within users and sessions reduce effective n and require cluster-robust analyses. Holdout sampling must be frozen before data acquisition: modality/mobile/desktop, session cold/warm, provider availability, long input, interruption, accessibility, native readback and backlog/persistence tasks. Missing outcomes count as failures unless a frozen exclusion rule says otherwise. No early stopping or changing threshold post hoc. Split training/development from holdout and use an independent adjudicator. C80's currently programmed method gives a *candidate signed-claims threshold*, never external-host certification.
